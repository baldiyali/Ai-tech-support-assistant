# No Sound, Microphone or Headset Problems

## Symptoms
- No sound from speakers or headset
- Others can't hear you in Teams/Zoom meetings
- Echo, crackling, or the wrong device is used

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check the volume isn't muted (taskbar speaker icon) and the headset's own mute switch/button is off.
2. Click the speaker icon in the taskbar and select the correct output device (your headset or speakers).
3. In your meeting app (Teams/Zoom), open Settings > Devices/Audio and choose the correct speaker and microphone; use "Make a test call".
4. Unplug the headset and plug it into a different USB port or audio jack; for Bluetooth headsets, disconnect and reconnect.
5. Check microphone privacy permissions: Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone, and allow access for your apps.
6. Run the audio troubleshooter: Settings > System > Troubleshoot > Other troubleshooters > Audio.
7. Restart the computer and re-test with another headset if one is available.

## When to Escalate
- No audio device appears at all in Sound settings (driver issue).
- The headset fails on multiple computers.

## Recommended Next Action
Reinstall/update the audio driver, check for meeting-app policies, and replace the headset if faulty.
