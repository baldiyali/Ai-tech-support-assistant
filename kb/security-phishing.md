# Suspicious Email, Phishing or Virus

## Symptoms
- An unexpected email asks you to click a link, open an attachment, or enter your password
- You clicked a suspicious link or opened an attachment
- Pop-ups, antivirus alerts, files renamed/encrypted, or contacts receiving strange emails from you

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Don't click any more links, open attachments, or reply to the suspicious message.
2. If you see files being encrypted or a ransom note, disconnect from the network immediately (turn off Wi-Fi / unplug the Ethernet cable) but leave the computer on.
3. Use your email's "Report phishing" button, or forward the email as an attachment to IT, then delete it.
4. If you entered your password anywhere, change it right away from a different, trusted device and approve only MFA prompts you started.
5. Run a full scan with Windows Security (Virus & threat protection > Scan options > Full scan).

## When to Escalate
- You entered your password or opened an attachment from a suspicious email: **always escalate.**
- Ransomware symptoms, or antivirus can't remove a threat.

## Recommended Next Action
Treat as a security incident: reset credentials and revoke sessions, check sign-in logs and mailbox rules, isolate and scan or reimage the device, and warn other employees about the campaign.
