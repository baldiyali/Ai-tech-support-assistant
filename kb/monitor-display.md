# Monitor or Display Problems

## Symptoms
- Monitor says "No signal" or stays black
- Screen flickers, has lines, or the wrong resolution
- Second monitor not detected

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Make sure the monitor is powered on (power light on) and its power cable is connected.
2. Reseat the video cable (HDMI/DisplayPort/USB-C) at both the monitor and the computer or docking station.
3. Use the monitor's menu buttons to select the correct input source (HDMI 1, HDMI 2, DisplayPort, etc.).
4. Press Win+P and choose "Extend" or "Duplicate" to make Windows detect the external display.
5. Go to Settings > System > Display, click "Detect", and set the recommended resolution and scale.
6. Try a different cable or a different port on the computer/dock. If you use a docking station, unplug the dock's power for 10 seconds and reconnect.
7. Update the graphics driver: Device Manager > Display adapters > right-click > Update driver.

## When to Escalate
- The built-in laptop screen is cracked, shows lines, or is black while an external monitor works.
- The monitor doesn't power on at all with a known-good power cable.

## Recommended Next Action
Swap in a known-good monitor/cable/dock to isolate the faulty part; replace hardware or repair the laptop screen as needed.
