# Computer Won't Turn On

## Symptoms
- Pressing the power button does nothing (no lights, no fans, no sound)
- Power light turns on but the screen stays black
- Laptop only works while plugged in, or not at all

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check that the power cable is firmly connected at both ends (computer and wall). For laptops, confirm the charger's LED is lit.
2. Plug the computer directly into a known-working wall outlet (test the outlet with a lamp or phone charger). Bypass any power strip or surge protector.
3. For desktops, check the power supply switch on the back of the tower is set to "I" (on), not "O" (off).
4. Perform a power drain: unplug the power cable (and remove the laptop battery if it is removable), then hold the power button for 30 seconds. Reconnect power and try again.
5. Disconnect all external devices (USB drives, docks, printers, extra monitors) and try powering on with only the power cable connected.
6. If the power light comes on but the screen is black, see the "Computer Turns On but Screen Stays Black" guide (connect an external monitor to rule out a broken screen).
7. For laptops, try a different compatible charger if one is available in the office.

## When to Escalate
- You smell burning, see smoke/sparks, or the device is physically damaged or wet: **unplug it immediately and do not try again.**
- No lights or fans at all after all steps: likely a failed power supply, battery, or motherboard.

## Recommended Next Action
Hardware inspection by a technician: test the power supply/charger and battery, and check the motherboard. Provide a loaner device if the user can't work.
