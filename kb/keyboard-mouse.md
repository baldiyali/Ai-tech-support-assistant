# Keyboard or Mouse Not Working

## Symptoms
- Keyboard or mouse doesn't respond
- Cursor jumps, lags, or some keys don't work
- Wireless/Bluetooth device keeps disconnecting

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. For wired devices, unplug the USB cable and plug it into a different USB port directly on the computer (not through a hub).
2. For wireless devices, replace or recharge the batteries and make sure the device's power switch is on.
3. Re-plug the wireless USB receiver (dongle), or for Bluetooth, remove the device in Settings > Bluetooth & devices and pair it again.
4. Clean the mouse sensor and keyboard (turn the keyboard upside down and gently shake out debris). Use a mouse pad on glossy surfaces.
5. Check that Filter Keys / Sticky Keys aren't enabled: Settings > Accessibility > Keyboard. Check NumLock if the number pad doesn't work.
6. For laptop touchpads, make sure the touchpad isn't disabled (often a function key such as F6/F9, or Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad).
7. Restart the computer, then test the device on another computer to see if the device itself has failed.

## When to Escalate
- The device fails on multiple computers (faulty hardware).
- The built-in laptop keyboard or touchpad stopped working.

## Recommended Next Action
Replace the faulty keyboard/mouse from spare stock; for built-in laptop input devices, check drivers then arrange a hardware repair.
